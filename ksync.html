The problem

Professional media sync is priced out of reach for most artists and institutions.

Coordinating video, lighting, sound, and automation across multiple displays currently costs €500 to €8,000+ per node — plus a technician day-rate to set it up. And every ambitious venue ends up trapped: reinventing the same fragile solution from scratch with expensive software like QLab, at the cost of weeks and thousands of euros.

€350 vs €1,167+ 3-year cost of a BrightSign node

Professional sync should be a tool anyone can afford to keep.

Independent artists, smaller museums and galleries, universities, and event organizers are shut out of display-synchronized work by pricing built for enterprise budgets. kSync exists to change that — with hardware that costs a fraction of the alternatives and none of the lock-in.

We want sync to be the default, not the privilege.

More than a media player. A conductor for your whole environment.

kSync is a dedicated, stripped-down Linux appliance. One web-based timeline orchestrates video, lighting, sound, and physical automation — everything scheduled against a single accurate media clock.

  • Video Frame-accurate synchronized playback across any number of displays.
  • Lighting Trigger DMX and Art-Net cues in time with the picture.
  • Sound Send OSC and MIDI to spatial audio and synthesizers.
  • Motion Control motors, relays, and kinetic sculpture via Arduino & serial.
  • Web Fire HTTP and WebSocket messages to APIs and external systems.

Deployed in minutes. No IT department needed.

Provisioning a node is as simple as plugging in a USB drive with your media and a config file. The device boots, finds its role, and starts syncing — a genuinely zero-config install, not "easy setup" marketing.

Each node is an optimized Raspberry Pi in a custom enclosure: energy-efficient, silent, and serviced with open-source tooling your own team can maintain.

Open source isn't a feature. It's the point.

You own your installation, your data, and your hardware forever. No forced upgrades, no subscription creep, no vendor that can hold your show hostage.
